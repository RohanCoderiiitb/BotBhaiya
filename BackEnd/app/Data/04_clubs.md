# Clubs at IIIT-Bangalore

IIIT-B has a vibrant club culture covering arts, sports, technology, literature, and social service.  
Here’s the complete list with descriptions, activities, and contact details.

---

## Cultural & Creative Clubs

### Parvaaz – The Theatre Club
- Known for gripping stage plays, mime performances, street plays, and theatre productions.
- Actively participates in inter-college festivals and wins multiple accolades.
- Also engages in social causes: collaborates with the Food Committee to raise awareness about food wastage and with the Internal Complaints Committee to fight sexual harassment.
- **Contact:** parvaaz@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@parvaaz_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/parvaaz_iiitb/)

### Impulse – The Dance Club
- Hosts flash mobs, “Rhythm Rumble” dance battles, and performances at Foundation Day.
- Open to beginners and experienced dancers.
- First-year troupe “Kanya Rasi” performed at Infin8 fest.
- **Contact:** impulse.danceclub@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@impulse_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/impulse_iiitb/)

### 8Bit – The College Magazine Club
- Student-run magazine featuring creative writing, art, design, and humor.
- Hosts events, design marathons, and editing challenges.
- Self-proclaimed “best club” on campus.
- **Contact:** 8bit@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@8bit_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/8bit_iiitb/)  
- **Website:** [8bit.iiitb.net](https://8bit.iiitb.net)

### Symphony – The Music Club
- Brings together musicians for performances at college events and competitions.
- Organizes “Battle of the Bands” and casual jamming sessions.
- **Contact:** symphony@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@symphony_iiitb_](https://www.instagram.com/symphony_iiitb_/)

### Vinci – The Art Club
- Hosts art competitions, workshops, and creative sessions.
- Activities include rock painting, t-shirt art, sketching, and digital art.
- **Contact:** art.club@iiitb.org  
- **Instagram:** [@vinci_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/vinci_iiitb/)

### DebSoc – The Debate Society
- Organizes debates (Asian Parliamentary format), public speaking sessions, and interview prep workshops.
- Aims to improve civic awareness and communication skills.
- **Contact:** debsoc@iiitb.org  
- **Instagram:** [@debsoc_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/debsoc_iiitb/)

### Chhayachitra – The Photography Club
- Captures events, fests, and candid campus life.
- Most handbook images are credited to this club.
- **Contact:** chhayachitra@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@chhayachitra_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/chhayachitra_iiitb/)

### Pioneers – The Math Club
- Discusses advanced math topics, holds problem-solving sessions, and organizes contests.
- Ideal for mathematics enthusiasts.
- **No specific Instagram link given in handbook**

### Turiya – Spirituality & Wellness Club
- Promotes mental wellness, time management, and spirituality.
- Collaborates with ISKCON Bangalore for meditation sessions.
- Hosts events like Kalaamrutam (art related to Lord Vishnu) and Aitihaasika (quiz on Indian epics).
- **Contact:** turiya.club@iiitb.org  
- **Instagram:** [@turiyaclub_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/turiyaclub_iiitb/)

### Aikyam – Social Service Club
- Organizes visits to orphanages, donation drives, and awareness events.
- **Contact:** aikyam@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@nss_unit_aikyam](https://www.instagram.com/nss_unit_aikyam/)

### Shatranj – The Chess Club
- Encourages chess playing through meetups, workshops, and tournaments.
- Open to beginners and experts alike.

### Quiz Society
- Conducts biweekly quizzes with prizes.
- No exclusive membership — open to all.
- **Contact:** quizsoc@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@quizsoc_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/quizsoc_iiitb/)

## Technical Clubs

### Zense – Developers' Community
- Oldest tech club, known for hackathons, CTFs, and workshops.
- Hosts “Hacknite” for first-year students in WebDev, AI/ML, Data Science, GameDev, and more.
- **Contact:** zense@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Website:** [zense.co.in](http://zense.co.in/)  
- **Instagram:** [@zense.iiitb](https://zense.iiitb)

### GDSC – Google Developer Student Club
- Focuses on Google technologies and peer-to-peer learning.
- Conducts workshops, speaker sessions, and projects.
- **Instagram:** [@gdsc_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/gdsc_iiitb/)

### Lean-In Club
- Part of the global Lean-In movement for women empowerment.
- Workshops, networking, and career discussions.
- **Contact:** leanin@iiitb.ac.in

### CAMP – Competitive Programming Club
- Led by ACM-ICPC world finalists.
- Holds contests, workshops, and problem-solving sessions.

### Astronomy Club
- Stargazing sessions, workshops, and discussions on astronomy.

### MOSFIT – VLSI Club
- Focuses on Very Large Scale Integration (chip design).
- Workshops, industry insights, and project mentorship.
- **Contact:** VLSI.club@iiitb.ac.in
