# IIIT Bangalore B.Tech. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) — Curriculum Summary (Batch 2024–2028)

**Version:** 1.0 (Approved May 31, 2024, Senate Meeting 97)  
**Focus:** Evolving computing; early skill-oriented courses; options for Honours and Minor.  
**Total Credits:** Minimum 154 (8 semesters).  
**Guiding Principles:**  
- 4-year benchmark structure.  
- AICTE flexibility integrated.  
- Encourages deep expertise and breadth.  
- Early introduction of discipline courses.

---

## 4. Credit & Grading System

- **Format:** L:T:P:C — where Lecture (L) and Tutorial (T) require 1 hour per week per credit, and Practical (P) requires 2 hours per week per credit.  
- **Grading:** 4-point CGPA scale (A = 4.00, F = 0.00; S/X for non-credit courses).  

**Course Categories:**  
- Engineering Core: Foundational for all B.Tech. students.  
- Branch Core: DSAI-specific foundational courses.  
- Elective: Choice-based; minimum required.  
- Branch Elective: DSAI-specific advanced courses.  
- Open Elective: Courses from other departments, prerequisites may apply.  
- Project Elective (PE): 4-credit experiential learning project.  
- Reading Elective (RE): 4-credit research reading course.  
- Internship: Mandatory industry or academic project.

**Multi-disciplinary Components:**  
- Engineering: CS, ECE, DS, Robotics, AI.  
- Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS): English, Communication, Economics, Digital Society, Product Management, Ethics.  
- Creative Arts: Theatre, Music, Art workshops.  
- Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS): Yoga.  
- Healthcare: Assistive technology projects.

---

## 5. Non-Credit Courses

- **Physical Education (PE1/PE2):** Daily exercise, games, yoga, or gardening.  
- **Value Added Learning Program (VALP):** Induction program, Environmental Science, Indian Constitution, Essence of IKS. Includes Music, Art, Dance, Life Skills through on-campus activities or MOOCs.

---

## 6. Degree Variants

- **B.Tech. DSAI (Default):** Meets all curriculum norms.  
- **B.Tech. (Hons.):** +20 credits in specialization (DSAI Algorithms or Data Domains); CGPA ≥ 3.5. Maximum 2 PE/RE allowed; electives cannot be branch courses.  
- **B.Tech. with Minor:** +20 credits from another department (VLSI, SSY, TCS, DT). Maximum 2 PE/RE allowed.

## 10. Suggested Semester Sequence

**Semester 1 (22 credits):** Math-1, Math-2, Introduction to Computer Architecture & OS, Programming 1A (C) — 2 credits, Programming 1B (Python) — 2 credits, English, Economics-1, PE1 (non-credit).  
**Semester 2 (20 credits):** Math-3, Statistical Machine Learning, Data Structures & Algorithms, Data Structures Lab, Database Systems (theory + lab), Statistics for Data Science, Economics-2, PE2 (non-credit).  
**Semester 3 (24 credits):** Foundations of AI, Neural Networks & Deep Learning, Math-4, Physics-1, Signals & Systems, Discrete Mathematics.  
**Semester 4 (20 credits):** Design & Analysis of Algorithms, Data & Society, Data Modeling, Advanced Deep Learning, Data Visualization, Technical Communication.  
**Semester 5 (20 credits):** Computational Learning Theory, Reinforcement Learning, AI Ethics, Big Data Systems, Machine Learning Production Engineering.  
**Semester 6 (16 credits):** Electives 1–4.  
**Semester 7 (20 credits):** Electives 5–8 (document lists 16 credits, but likely a typo).  
**Semester 8 (12 credits):** Project/Thesis/Internship.
