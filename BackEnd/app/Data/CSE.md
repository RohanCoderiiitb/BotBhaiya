# IIIT Bangalore B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — Curriculum Summary (Batch 2024–2028)

**Version:** 1.0 (Approved May 31, 2024, Senate Meeting 97)  
**Focus:** Evolving computing; early skill-oriented courses; options for Honours and Minor.  
**Total Credits:** Minimum 150 (8 semesters).  
**Guiding Principles:**  
- 4-year benchmark structure.  
- AICTE flexibility integrated.  
- Encourages both deep expertise and breadth.  
- Early introduction of CSE discipline courses.

---

- **Founded:** 1998 (Deemed University).  
- **Vision:** Attain global IT leadership through education, research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.  
- **Mission:** Transform lives and society via information technology.

## 2. Graduate Attributes (GAs)

**General Graduate Attributes** — At graduation, a student should have:  
- G1: Ability to identify and analyze problems through design thinking.  
- G2: Proficiency in applying mathematics for problem-solving.  
- G3: Ability to work effectively in multicultural teams.  
- G4: Effective communication skills.  
- G5: Commitment to self-learning and lifelong learning.  
- G6: Ethical responsibility in professional contexts.  
- G7: Ability to conduct small research projects.  
- G8: Entrepreneurial mindset.  
- G9: Understanding of impacts on economy, society, and environment.  
- G10: Emotional intelligence and cultural values.

**Discipline-specific GAs for CSE:**  
- CS1: Proficiency in at least two programming languages.  
- CS2: Ability to use algorithms and data structures for efficient solutions.  
- CS3: Understanding of architecture, operating systems, and distributed systems performance.  
- CS4: Understanding of theoretical foundations and limits.  
- CS5: Proficiency in data analysis techniques.  
- CS6 (Advanced): Ability to design and evaluate systems using tools.  
- CS7 (Advanced): Full-stack application development with a technology stack.  
- CS8 (Advanced): Mastery of advanced techniques in specialized areas.  
- CS9 (Advanced): Expertise in cloud and DevOps.

## 4. Credit & Grading System

- **Format:** L:T:P:C — where Lecture (L) and Tutorial (T) require 1 hour per week per credit, and Practical (P) requires 2 hours per week per credit.  
- **Grading:** 4-point CGPA scale (A = 4.00, F = 0.00; S/X for non-credit courses).  

**Course Categories:**  
- Engineering Core: Foundational for all B.Tech. students.  
- Branch Core: CSE-specific foundational courses.  
- Elective: Choice-based; minimum required.  
- Branch Elective: CSE-specific courses from Theoretical Computer Science (TCS) and Systems Software (SSY) pools.  
- Open Elective: Courses from other departments, prerequisites may apply.  
- Project Elective (PE): 4-credit experiential learning project.  
- Reading Elective (RE): 4-credit research reading course.  
- Internship: Mandatory industry or academic project.

**Multi-disciplinary Components:**  
- Engineering: CS, ECE, DS, AI, Robotics.  
- Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS): English, Communication, Economics, Digital Society, Product Management, Ethics.  
- Creative Arts: Theatre, Music, Art workshops.  
- Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS): Yoga.  
- Healthcare: Assistive technology projects.

## 5. Non-Credit Courses

- **Physical Education (PE1/PE2):** Daily exercise, games, yoga, or gardening.  
- **Value Added Learning Program (VALP):** Induction program, Environmental Science, Indian Constitution, Essence of IKS. Includes Music, Art, Dance, Life Skills through on-campus activities or MOOCs.

---

## 6. Degree Variants

- **B.Tech. CSE (Default):** Meets all curriculum norms.  
- **B.Tech. (Hons.):** +20 credits in a specialization (TCS or SSY); CGPA ≥ 3.5. Maximum 2 PE/RE allowed; electives cannot be branch courses.  
- **B.Tech. with Minor:** +20 credits from another department (VLSI, NC, DT, AIML). Maximum 2 PE/RE allowed.

## 10. Suggested Semester Sequence

**Semester 1 (18 credits):** Math-1, Programming 1A (C), Programming 1B (Python), Digital Design, PE1 (non-credit), English, Economics-1, Math-2.  
**Semester 2 (18 credits):** Math-3, Computer Architecture, Data Structures & Algorithms (theory + lab), Computer Networks, Economics-2, PE2 (non-credit).  
**Semester 3 (22 credits):** Technical Communication, Programming 2A (C++), Programming 2B (Java), Physics Theory & Lab, Math-4, Signals & Systems, Discrete Mathematics.  
**Semester 4 (20 credits):** Operating Systems Theory & Lab, Design & Analysis of Algorithms, Database Systems Theory & Lab, Social Science Core, Elective 1.  
**Semester 5 (20 credits):** Automata Theory & Computability, Software Engineering Theory & Lab, Electives 2–4.  
**Semester 6 (20 credits):** Electives 5–9.  
**Semester 7 (20 credits):** Electives 10–14 (including HSS elective).  
**Semester 8 (12 credits):** Project/Thesis/Internship.

---

**Note:** One HSS elective is taken in Semesters 4–7.
