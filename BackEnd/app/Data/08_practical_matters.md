# Practical Matters for First-Year Students

---

## Things to Bring
- **Essentials:** Clothing, toiletries, sports shoes, formal wear, calculator, water bottle, locks.
- **Electronics:** Laptop with charger, headphones, power bank.
- **Room Items:** Mattress, pillow, bed sheets, blankets, buckets, mugs, hangers, extension cords.
- **Miscellaneous:** Medicines, stationary kit, ID proofs.
- **Tip:** D-Mart (1.5 km from campus) is a convenient place to buy bulky items after arrival.

---

## Laptop Guide
- **Processor:** Intel i5/i7 or AMD Ryzen 5/7.
- **RAM:** Minimum 8 GB (16 GB recommended).
- **Storage:** SSD preferred for speed; HDD for extra storage.
- **Graphics:** Integrated for academics; dedicated GPU for game development, design, or ML.
- **OS Recommendations:**  
  - Windows — widely supported, good for general use.  
  - Linux — great for programming, server tools.  
  - MacOS — especially good for design, app dev; buy with student discount.

---

## Discipline Rules
- Maintain silence in academic areas.
- No smoking, alcohol, or drugs on campus.
- Ragging is prohibited under law — strict action will be taken.
- Keep campus and hostels clean.
- Carry your ID card at all times.

---

## Visitor Policy
- Visiting hours: **10 AM – 7 PM**.
- Visitors are not allowed in hostel rooms.
- Guests must sign in at the security desk.

---

## Pro Tips
- Join at least one club early on — helps you make friends.
- Explore Bangalore’s eateries, parks, and tech scene.
- Don’t overload yourself with too many commitments in the first semester.
