# Festivals at IIIT-Bangalore

IIIT-B celebrates a wide variety of festivals to reflect its diverse student body.  
These events combine tradition with the energy of youth.

---

## Diwali
- Campus decorated with diyas and rangolis.
- Cultural performances, fireworks, and traditional sweets.
- Students dress in ethnic attire.

## Holi
- Celebrated with colors, music, and snacks.
- Uses herbal colors to avoid harm to skin and environment.
- Event concludes with DJ and dance.

## Ganesh Chaturthi
- Installation of Lord Ganesha idol on campus.
- Daily pooja and bhajans for a week.
- Ends with a visarjan procession.

## Onam
- Celebrates Kerala’s harvest festival.
- Pookolam (floral carpet) making competition.
- Tug-of-war and cultural programs.
- Onam Sadya (traditional Kerala meal) served.

## Harvest Festivals
- **Lohri:** Bonfire, folk songs, traditional sweets.
- **Makar Sankranti:** Kite flying event.
- **Pongal:** Cooking and sharing the traditional sweet dish.

## Karnataka Rajyotsava
- Commemorates Karnataka state formation.
- Folk dance performances and Kannada literature quizzes.
- Special Kannadiga lunch served in the mess.
