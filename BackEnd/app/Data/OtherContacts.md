## Hostel Gate Pass Approval Contacts

### Men's Hostel Gate Approval
- **Email:** mh-gate-approval@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Purpose:**  
  - **Mandatory** email for male hostel residents when leaving campus for personal or overnight trips.  
  - Used to get formal gate pass approval from hostel administration.  
  - Include details: name, roll number, reason for leaving, destination, and expected return time/date.

### Women's Hostel Gate Approval
- **Email:** wh-gate-approval@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Purpose:**  
  - **Mandatory** email for female hostel residents when stepping out of campus after hours or for overnight trips.  
  - Approval ensures safety and records entry/exit.  
  - Include complete trip details: name, roll number, reason, destination, return date/time, and guardian contact (if applicable).

### Facility Manager
- **Email:** facilitymanager@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Purpose:**
