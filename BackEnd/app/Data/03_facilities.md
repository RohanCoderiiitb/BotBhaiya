# College Life and Facilities

## Food & Dining
- Common mess and canteen for all students.
- Four meals daily: breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner.
- Chicken option thrice a week for dinner, separate Jain meals.
- Mess menu rotates every two weeks.
- Canteen open until 2 AM; reopens at 10 AM.

## Parking
- Two parking locations: Front Gate & Bhaskara Basement.

## JustVend
- 24x7 vending machine.
- Use the JustVend app to order; restocked regularly.

## Recreational Spaces
- Couches and informal spaces for group projects.
- Outdoor badminton court on Bhaskara’s first floor.
- Common rooms and pantry in Lilavati.
- Pond area for relaxation after classes.

## Laundry
- Laundry room in Bhaskara’s basement with washing machines and dryers.
- 24-hour turnaround for wash service.
- ₹100 for instant drying via dryer.
- Ironing service available per garment.
- College-issued laundry card required.

## Safety
- Fire alarms, smoke detectors, sprinklers.
- Clearly marked emergency exits.
- Daily cleaning of hostel washrooms.

## Sports Facilities
- General-purpose field (football, cricket).
- Indoor games (chess, carrom, table tennis) in MPH.
- Separate gyms for men (Shape Up) and women (Calorie Bee).
- Basketball, volleyball, throwball, badminton courts.
- Gym timings:  
  - Mon–Sat: 6 AM–9 AM & 3:30 PM–9 PM  
  - Closed on Sundays.

## Medical Facilities
- On-campus doctors:  
  - Dr. Sachidanandam Muniswamy (Tue, Fri, Sat: 6–9 PM)  
  - Dr. Bharathi P (Mon, Wed, Thu: 11:30 AM–2 PM)  
- On-campus para-medic.
- Apollo Pharmacy nearby.
- **YourDOST** counseling services (online & in-person).
- Tie-up with Kauvery Hospital for emergency ambulance (free service).
- Medical insurance: ₹2 lakh/year for hospital stays >24 hours.
