**Version:** 1.0 (Approved May 31, 2024, Senate Meeting 97)  
**Focus:** Low-power communication, SoC design; aligned with India's semiconductor development push.

- 4-year benchmark curriculum  
- AICTE flexibility integrated

## 4. Credit & Grading System

- **Format:** L:T:P:C  
  - L = Lecture hours per week per credit  
  - T = Tutorial hours per week per credit  
  - P = 2 hours of practicals per credit  
- **Grading:** 4-point CGPA scale (A = 4.00, F = 0.00; S/X for non-credit courses).  

**Course Categories:**
- Engineering Core: Foundational for all B.Tech. students.  
- Branch Core: ECE-specific foundational courses.  
- Elective: Choice-based, minimum requirement to be fulfilled.  
- Branch Elective: ECE-specific electives (VLSI and Networking & Communication pools).  
- Open Elective: Cross-departmental, prerequisites may apply.  
- Project Elective (PE): 4-credit experiential learning project.  
- Reading Elective (RE): 4-credit research-focused reading course.  
- Internship: Mandatory industry or academic project.

**Multi-Disciplinary Components:**
- Engineering: CS, ECE, DS, AI, Robotics.  
- HSS: English, Communication, Economics, Digital Society, Ethics.  
- Creative Arts: Theatre, Music, Art workshops.  
- IKS: Yoga practice.  
- Healthcare: Assistive technology projects.

## 5. Non-Credit Courses

## 6. Degree Variants

- **B.Tech. ECE (Default):** Meets all curriculum norms.  
- **B.Tech. (Hons.):** +20 credits in specialization (VLSI or Networking & Communication), CGPA ≥ 3.5. Maximum 2 PE/RE; electives cannot be branch courses.  
- **B.Tech. with Minor:** +20 credits from another department (TCS, SSY, AIML, DT). Maximum 2 PE/RE.

## 10. Sequencing Overview

**Semester 1:** Math 1, Programming 1A, Programming 1B, Digital Design, PE1 (0 cr), English, Economics 1, Math 2.  
**Semester 2:** Computer Architecture, Data Structures & Algorithms, Data Structures Lab, Computer Networks, Math 3, PE2 (0 cr), Economics 3.  
**Semester 3:** Programming 2A, Microcontroller Programming, Physics for ECE, Signals & Systems, Electrical Circuits & Network Analysis, Electrical Circuits Lab, Technical Communication.  
**Semester 4:** Operating Systems Theory & Lab, Advanced Operating Systems Theory & Lab, Analog Circuits Theory & Lab, Signal Processing, Principles of Communication Theory & Lab, HSS Core.  
**Semester 5–7:** Control Theory, Advanced Digital Communication Theory & Lab, Branch electives, Open electives.  
**Semester 8:** Project/Thesis/Internship.
