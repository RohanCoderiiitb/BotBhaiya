# Committees at IIIT-Bangalore

Committees are student-led or joint student–faculty groups that manage campus operations, events, and policy.

---

## Sports Committee
- Organizes events like Spandan, Umang, and Premier League.
- Maintains sports facilities and mediates ground usage disputes.
- **Contact:** sportscomm@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@sportscomm_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/sportscomm_iiitb/)

## Food Committee
- Acts as liaison for food-related issues.
- Works on improving quality and sustainability, reducing food waste.
- **Contact:** foodcommittee@iiitb.ac.in

## Branding Committee
- Promotes IIIT-B’s brand internally and externally.
- Runs “Bangalore on IT” public lecture series.
- **Contact:** branding@iiitb.ac.in

## Placement Committee
- Coordinates on-campus placements and internship processes.
- Provides preparation support for interviews.
- **Contact:** placement.committee@iiitb.ac.in

## Alumni Committee
- Connects current students with alumni.
- Organizes “Sangam” alumni meet.
- **Contact:** alumni.committee@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@iiitb_alumni](https://www.instagram.com/iiitb_alumni/)

## Gender Cell
- Promotes gender equality and justice.
- Conducts workshops, seminars, and inquiries into misconduct.
- **Website:** [Gender Cell Portal](https://iiitbgcellportal-glado8.vercel.app)

## Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
- Handles sexual harassment complaints as per UGC “Saksham” guidelines.
- Creates awareness on gender and safety.
- **Contact:** icc-iiitb@iiitb.ac.in

## Student Affairs Council (SAC)
- Elected body bridging students and administration.
- First point of contact for student policy discussions.
- **Contact:** sac@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@sac_iiitb](https://www.instagram.com/sac_iiitb/)

## Gym Committee
- Manages gym schedules, equipment maintenance.
- Recently doubled gym size.
- **Contact:** gymcommittee@iiitb.ac.in  
- **Instagram:** [@gymcommiiitb](https://www.instagram.com/gymcommiiitb/)

## Student Disciplinary Committee (SDC)
- Maintains safe, harmonious campus environment.
- Plays key role during fests to ensure smooth conduct.
