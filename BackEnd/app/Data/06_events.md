# Events at IIIT-Bangalore

IIIT-B hosts a diverse range of events — from academic summits to cultural extravaganzas and sports tournaments.  
These events give you opportunities to explore talents, meet industry leaders, and bond with peers.

---

## Infin8
- IIIT-B’s annual techno-cultural fest.
- Features plays, battle of the bands, dance performances, stand-up comedy, DJ nights.
- Attracts participation from colleges across India.
- **Highlights:** Rock Night, Fashion Show, Hackathons.

## Synergy
- Tech fest showcasing innovations from various clubs.
- Includes SYNMUN (Model United Nations), coding marathons, design challenges.
- Open to all batches, with high first-year participation.

## TEDx IIIT-Bangalore
- Independently organized TEDx conference.
- Speakers from tech, entrepreneurship, arts, social work.
- Live talks, networking breaks, and hands-on workshops.
- **Instagram:** [@tedxiiitbangalore](https://www.instagram.com/tedxiiitbangalore/)

## Confluence
- Annual entrepreneurship and leadership summit.
- Panel discussions, startup pitches, networking with venture capitalists.

## Umang
- Inter-college sports fest with competitions in cricket, basketball, football, badminton, table tennis, volleyball.
- Friendly yet competitive atmosphere.

## Premier League
- Inspired by IPL auction format.
- Students bid on players to form sports teams.
- Involves cricket, football, and basketball tournaments.

## Spandan
- Annual sports meet that also hosts alumni matches.
- Includes athletics and team games.
- Prize distribution and closing ceremony are major attractions.

## Sangam
- Alumni Day with cultural performances, panel discussions, and networking.
- Often coincides with Foundation Day.

## Miles4Meals
- Charity marathon in partnership with Akshaya Patra Foundation.
- Proceeds used to provide mid-day meals for underprivileged children.
- Open to public participation.
