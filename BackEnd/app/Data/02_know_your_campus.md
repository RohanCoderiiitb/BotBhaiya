# Know Your Campus

IIIT-Bangalore’s campus consists of multiple academic and residential blocks, each with unique purposes.

## Aryabhatta (Old Academic Block)
- Houses the **main reception**, **library**, **classrooms**, **labs**, **administration office**, **director’s office**, and **faculty offices**.
- You can approach faculty at any time for academic queries or advice.
- **Sports Room** — issue sports equipment here.
- **Coffee Cove** — serves coffee, tea, and beverages during college hours.
- Includes PhD scholars’ office and study spaces.

## Ramanujan (New Academic Block)
- Inaugurated in 2017.
- **Ground floor:** Computer labs and classrooms.
- **First floor:** Research labs (MINRO, SRL) and seminar halls.
- **2nd floor:** Sports Complex.
- **3rd floor:** Auditorium.
- The **Thinker’s Statue** outside is a popular photography spot.
- Additional Coffee Cove for students and faculty.

## Hostels

### Bhaskara (Boys)
- 70 triple-sharing rooms (first 2 years), 400 single rooms (after 2nd year).
- Well-ventilated rooms with two windows each.

### Visvesvaraya (Boys)
- 90 rooms: triple-sharing with attached bathroom + single rooms.
- Each student has a cupboard and study desk.

### Lilavati (Girls)
- Single rooms for all years (room change every 1–2 years).
- Two guards 24/7, daily housekeeping.
- **Calorie Bee** — girls’ gym located inside.

## Multi-Purpose Hall (MPH)
- Recreational hub located in Bhaskara’s ground floor.
- **Facilities:** Badminton court, table tennis, foosball, Shape Up gym (men), SAC room, music room, dance room.
- **8Bit Corner:** Dedicated area for studying or gaming.
